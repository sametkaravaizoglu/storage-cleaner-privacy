Privacy Policy — Storage Cleaner: Photo Cleanup

Last updated: November 23, 2026 · Developer: Ailurus fulgens · Contact: sametkaravaizoglu@gmail.com

Short version

  • Your photos and videos are analyzed only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • We do not have accounts, servers, or a database of your data.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

What the app accesses

Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android).

Used to find duplicate, similar, blurry and large media, screenshots, and WhatsApp media so you can

choose what to delete. Analysis results (file IDs, sizes, dates, image fingerprints) are stored in a local

database on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

On Android 14+ you may share only selected photos; the app then analyzes only those.

Deleting. Files are only deleted after you confirm in a system dialog. On Android 11+ they go to the

system trash for 30 days and can be restored. On Android 10 and earlier there is no system trash, and the

app tells you deletion is permanent before you confirm.

Data collected by third parties

Google AdMob (ads) may collect: device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate

location derived from IP, app interactions with ads, and diagnostics. This is used to show and measure ads

and to prevent fraud. Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), we ask for consent using Google's User Messaging

Platform before ads are personalized. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google Play Billing processes the optional "Remove ads" purchase. We receive only a purchase token,

never your payment details.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your photos for advertising.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

  • Revoke photo access anytime in Android Settings → Apps → Storage Cleaner → Permissions.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Buy "Remove ads" to stop all ad requests.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all locally stored analysis data.
